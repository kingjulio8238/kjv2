Humanoid Atlas is an open-source intelligence platform for the humanoid robotics industry. It lets you explore OEM ecosystems, trace component suppliers, surface geopolitical dependencies, and run AI-powered strategic analysis.

The project took off immediately after launch:

- **215,000+ views**
- **13,000+ site visits**

*Explore the atlas at [humanoids.fyi](https://www.humanoids.fyi).*

## What It Does

The platform combines interactive 3D point-cloud renderings of robotics hardware with a network graph that maps relationships across the entire humanoid supply chain. Users can drill into specific components - motors, actuators, batteries, sensors - compare OEM specs side by side, and run scenario models powered by AI.

Country-level analysis highlights where critical supply chain dependencies sit, making it a practical tool for investors, operators, and policymakers tracking the humanoid buildout.

Every humanoid robot needs joints that are compact, precise, and strong. Harmonic drives (aka strain wave gears) are the component that makes this possible - delivering high gear ratios with near-zero backlash in a package small enough to fit inside a robotic wrist. They are also the **single most expensive component in a rotary actuator at ~36% of cost**, and every major humanoid OEM on the planet depends on them.

The problem is that the United States is not producing enough locally. 

## Who Makes Them

There are only three main manufacturers of harmonic reducers in the world:

- **Harmonic Drive Systems (Japan)** - 20-25% global market share, supplies Tesla, Figure, Apptronik, Dexmate, Unitree, UBTECH, XPeng, AgiBot, Xiaomi, and EngineAI
- **Nabtesco (Japan)** - ~60% of the cycloidal reducer market (the primary alternative), supplies Boston Dynamics and Agility
- **Leaderdrive (China)** - the domestic Chinese supplier, already serving Fourier, Kepler, MagicLab, AgiBot, and Unitree

Japan and China. That's it. Only **12% of global machine tool manufacturers** even meet the technical requirements to produce these gears. Out of 15 OEMs tracked on [Humanoid Atlas](https://humanoids.fyi) , **13 have single-source risk** on reducers meaning one supply disruption away from a full production halt.

## The US Shortfall

The US is building humanoid robot factories at an unprecedented pace. Tesla is constructing a 1-2M sqft Optimus factory at Giga Texas targeting 10M units/yr. Figure's BotQ in San Jose is operational at 12K/yr capacity. Agility's RoboFab in Salem is running at 10K/yr. Hyundai is building a Metaplant in Savannah for 30K Atlas units/yr by 2028. Combined, US factories are targeting millions of robots per year by the end of the decade.

Every single one of those robots will need harmonic drives. And in today's current state every single harmonic drive will be imported.

Meanwhile, China is not waiting. Leaderdrive is already manufacturing domestically for Chinese OEMs - Fourier, Kepler, MagicLab, and others are sourcing reducers without ever crossing a border. China's humanoid OEMs shipped well over 7,000 units in 2025 across Unitree, AgiBot, UBTECH, Booster, and others, all with locally available reducer supply.

## Why This Matters

We've had this conversation before with semiconductors. The US designed the chips but outsourced fabrication until it became a national security problem. CHIPS Act was the response - tens of billions to reshore a capability we never should have lost.

Harmonic drives are the semiconductors of robotics. A single trade disruption with Japan halts every US humanoid production line overnight. No amount of AI model breakthroughs matter if the robot's joints can't be built. **Hardware sovereignty means owning the full stack - not just the brain, but the muscle.**

The US needs to mass manufacture harmonic drives locally. The window for the US is now, before the production targets go from thousands to millions and the dependency becomes irreversible.

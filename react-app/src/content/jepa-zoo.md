JEPA Zoo maps the entire JEPA family from the original Joint Embedding Predictive Architecture through every variant that has followed.
![JEPA Evolution Tree](/jepa-zoo-og.png)

## What It Does

- **Evolution Tree** - a timeline from 2022 to 2026 showing how each JEPA variant branched from its predecessors, color-coded by modality (vision, video, audio, 3D/spatial, robotics, physics, scaling, ...)

- **Architecture Walkthroughs** - step-by-step diagrams for each variant (I-JEPA, V-JEPA, Audio-JEPA, ACT-JEPA, ThinkJEPA, and more) breaking down how these variants are trained and used at inference 

- **Comparison View** - architecture comparisons so you can see exactly what changed between variants

*Explore it at [jepazoo.com](https://jepazoo.com)*
